PPT Slide 5 Architecture

Designed for Both Authorities & Communities

The 6 core pillars uniting real-time predictive intelligence with field-level disaster response.

01

AI Risk Assessment

Estimates location-wise landslide risk from multiple environmental factors (rainfall, slope, moisture, soil texture) using trained XGBoost and PyTorch models.

02

Interactive Risk Map

Visualizes Low, Moderate, and High risk zones across key North Eastern Region (NER) mountain corridors using Leaflet.js.

03

Early-Warning Alerts

Triggers automated location-based warnings and siren broadcasts when critical hydrological or slope movement thresholds are breached.

04

Authority Dashboard

Command center for SDMA, NDRF, and district commissioners to monitor high-vulnerability locations, deploy heavy equipment, and manage evacuation corridors.

05

Citizen Reporting

Enables local residents and first responders to submit geo-tagged observations, photo evidence of cracks, and ground seepage directly from the field.

06

Explainable View

Deconstructs the "black box" by showing the major environmental factors (SHAP attribution) behind the assigned risk level to foster trust with decision-makers.
